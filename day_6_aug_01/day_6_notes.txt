Event loop
==========
-javascript Event Loop allows to perform asynchronous operations even though it is single threaded.


CallStack: maintains the execution contexts, executes them in LIFO Order

Note: normal functions are added to callStack directly, but callback/async functions are not added to callstack directly, they have to wait

Functions waiting in the below queues are executed only after normal functions are executed
MicroTask Queue : promise callback, async , process.nexttick()
MacroTask Queue : setTimeout, setInterval


EventLoop : Keeps checking callstack, once callstack is free, assignes functions from queue to execute
